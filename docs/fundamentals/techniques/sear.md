# Sear

Source path: `/fundamental/technique/sear`

Cooking an ingredient in a pan over high heat.

## What are the fundamentals of searing?

Searing is the act of cooking an ingredient in a pan over very high heat to trigger the [Maillard Reaction](../reactions/maillard.md) and brown its exterior.

- Fats or oils are typically used in the pan as a thermal medium.
- Unlike [Saute](./saute.md), searing minimizes tossing or movement of ingredients to achieve a more crisp outer layer.

> Kitchen Tool: Pan

> Heat Type: Conduction w/ fat

> Pan Surface Temperature: 400°F/205°C+

> Food Reaction(s): [Maillard](../reactions/maillard.md), [Dehydration](../reactions/dehydration.md), [Coagulation](../reactions/coagulation.md)

### Example Foods

- Searing a steak
- Develop a crust on a smash burger
- Searing chicken on a flat top
