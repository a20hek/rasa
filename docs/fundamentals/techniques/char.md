# Char

Source path: `/fundamental/technique/char`

Lightly burning an ingredient with high heat.

## What are the fundamentals of charring?

Charring is a technique where you intentionally burn an ingredient, or part of an ingredient, in a controlled manner using direct, high heat. This can be done in a pan, grill, oven, or with direct flame.

- Charring is a great way to achieve texture & color contrast and a controlled bitter flavor in a dish.

Why?

- Charred, carbonized bits of food get you complex flavors that taste like it was cooked over a charcoal fire, even if it was not.

> Kitchen Tool: Pan, Grill, Torch, Oven

> Heat Type: Radiation or Conduction

> Temperature: 400-500°F/204-260°C

> Food Reaction(s): [Maillard](../reactions/maillard.md), [Dehydration](../reactions/dehydration.md)

### Example Foods

- Deeply roasted carrots
- Seared onions
- Grill marks on a chicken breast
