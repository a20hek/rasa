# Shallow Fry

Source path: `/fundamental/technique/shallow-fry`

Cooking in a shallow layer of hot oil or fat.

## Shallow fry

Shallow frying uses a thin layer of fat or oil in a shallow cooking vessel to cook one side of an ingredient at a time. Unlike [Deep Fry](./deep-fry.md), the ingredients never become fully submerged in oil.

- Usually, frying is done to achieve texture & browning. It is a great method to get a crispy, golden brown exterior that contrasts against a gently cooked interior.

What about air frying?

Air frying is just convection cooking. See [Roast](./roast.md).

> Kitchen Tool: Pan

> Heat Type: Conduction w/ fat

> Oil Temperature: 325-375°F/160-190°C

> Food Reaction(s): [Maillard](../reactions/maillard.md), [Coagulation](../reactions/coagulation.md), [Dehydration](../reactions/dehydration.md)

### Example Foods

- Chiles rellenos
- Fried plantains
- Pan fried schnitzel
