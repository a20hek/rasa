# Simmer

Source path: `/fundamental/technique/simmer`

Gently cooking food in lightly bubbling liquid.

## What are the fundamentals of simmering?

Simmering is a cooking technique where food is cooked in lightly bubbling liquid at a temperature range between 180-212°F/82-100°C at sea level.

- Water is a consistent and dependent temperature regulator. A gentle simmer, small bubbles and not a rolling boil, ensures your food cooks within a predictable temperature range.

Simmering is a means of evaporation while using an uncovered pan or pot, which will concentrate the flavors of a liquid. See [Reduce](./reduce.md).

> Kitchen Tool: Pot

> Heat Type: Conduction w/ water

> Temperature: <180-212°F/82-100°C

> Food Reaction(s): [Dehydration](../reactions/dehydration.md), [Caramelization](../reactions/caramelization.md), [Coagulation](../reactions/coagulation.md)

### Example Foods

- Teriyaki sauce
- Vodka sauce
- Ragu bolognese
