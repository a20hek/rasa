# Microwave

Source path: `/fundamental/technique/microwave`

Using electromagnetic radiation to warm foods quickly.

## What are the fundamentals of microwaving?

Microwaving is a cooking method that uses electromagnetic waves to heat and cook food quickly and efficiently.

- Microwaves penetrate the food and cause water molecules to vibrate, producing heat that cooks the food from the inside out.
- This method is useful for reheating leftovers, cooking small portions, melting or softening ingredients, and defrosting frozen items.

Moist foods cook better and faster in the microwave than dry ingredients because the cooking method requires water content within the food to produce heat.

> Kitchen Tool: Microwave oven

> Heat Type: Electromagnetic radiation

> Temperature: Varies depending on microwave power

> Food Reaction(s): [Coagulation](../reactions/coagulation.md), Evaporation, [Caramelization](../reactions/caramelization.md)

### Example Foods

- Reheated leftovers
- Microwave popcorn
- Steamed vegetables
- Microwave "baked" potato
- Softened butter
- Melted chocolate
