Source path: `/discover/collection/help-us-update-our-food-photography`

Help Us Update Our Food Photography Exciting development! We can now update our website photography with user-submitted photos.

If you want to be featured with photo credit and glory, you can now plate up a Cook Well recipe and then follow our photography guide to submit a photo, which is linked on every recipe page by the photographer's name.

We especially need help with the following recipes, which could use an image refresh.
