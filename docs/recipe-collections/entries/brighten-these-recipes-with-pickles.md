Source path: `/discover/collection/brighten-these-recipes-with-pickles`

Brighten These Recipes With Pickles Almost any recipe can benefit from a pickled vegetable garnish, which adds acidity and amplifies flavors.

These recipes come alive with a few pickled cucumbers, marinated carrot shreds, or a spoonful of pickled onions .
