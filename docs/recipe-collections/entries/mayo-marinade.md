# Mayo marinade

Source path: `/discover/collection/mayo-marinade`

Mayo marinade As covered in many videos and newsletters, the mayo marinade is a great technique to make better seared protein. You can try it in these recipes below.

If you struggle to get good color or browning on your proteins, this will make searing foolproof.

This works with any flavorings or protein, even hearty vegetables and tofu!
