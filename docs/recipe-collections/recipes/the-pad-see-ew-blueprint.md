## Ingredients

### 1/2 part sauce

- soy sauce - equal amount / 25 g
- dark soy sauce - equal amount / 25 g
- oyster sauce - equal amount / 25 g
- sugar - equal amount / 25 g

### 1/8 part aromatics

- garlic, thinly sliced - 3-4 cloves
- white pepper - to taste
- salt - to taste
- cooking oil - for stir frying

### 1 part noodles

- wide rice noodles - ~1/2 lb / ~225 g

### 1-2 parts protein & vegetables

- protein of choice, thinly sliced - ~1/2 lb / ~225 g
- chinese broccoli, roughly chopped - ~1/2 lb / ~225 g
- eggs, beaten - 1-2

## Method

### Step 1: Prep noodles & sauce

Cook or soak the noodles according to package directions, drain, and set aside. Mix all sauce components until the sugar dissolves.

### Step 2: Stir fry the protein & vegetables

In batches, use a bit of oil and high heat to stir fry the egg, sliced protein, and vegetables until cooked through and lightly browned. Add each batch to a large bowl on this side.

### Step 3: Bring it together

Lastly, bloom the garlic in a bit of oil. Add the drained noodles and some sauce to the pan, which should start sizzling and charring a bit. Add the reserved vegetables and proteins and stir fry to combine.

### Step 4: Adjust & serve

If the noodles are dry, add more sauce as needed. Taste and season with white pepper and salt, if needed. Enjoy warm.
