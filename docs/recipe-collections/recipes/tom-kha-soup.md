# Tom Kha Soup

Source path: `/recipe/tom-kha-soup`

A Thai soup full of flavor.

## Metadata

- Author: Keith McBrayer
- Yield: 2-4 servings
- Time: 45 mins
- Tags: Level Up, Curious, Under 1 Hour
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/a57e233512ec0fe6a3ee213a931ac6e6f5030731-3543x3543.jpg`

## Ingredients

### Aromatic base broth

- chicken broth - 700 g
- galangal root, thinly sliced - 40 g
- lemongrass - 40 g
- red thai chiles, chopped - 3-5

### Soup components

- chicken thighs, boneless, skinless - 454 g
- mushrooms, thinly sliced - 225 g
- coconut milk - 240 g
- fish sauce - 60 g
- lime juice - ~ 60 g / to taste

### Toppings

- limes
- cilantro

## Method

### Step 1: Make the broth

In a large pot, add all aromatic base broth components and bring to a simmer. Reduce heat and simmer for 5-10 minutes to infuse aromatics into the broth.

### Step 2: Simmer the chicken & mushrooms

Add the chicken and mushrooms to the broth and simmer for 20 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through.

### Step 3: Finish the soup

Add in the coconut milk and fish sauce and simmer for 1 more minute, then turn off heat.

Taste and slowly add in lime juice, to taste, for acidity. If the soup needs more salt, add in additional fish sauce.

### Step 4: Garnish & serve

You can fish out the aromatics, or just and eat around them. The galangal and chilis slices are edible, but the lemongrass will be tough and fibrous.

Serve in bowls and optionally garnish with chopped cilantro and more lime wedges.
