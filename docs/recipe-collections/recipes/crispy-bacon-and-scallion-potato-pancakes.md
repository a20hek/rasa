# Crispy Bacon & Scallion Potato Pancakes

Source path: `/recipe/crispy-bacon-and-scallion-potato-pancakes`

A great way to repurpose leftovers.

## Ingredients

### Pancake components

- mashed potatoes - 400 g
- eggs - 1
- panko bread crumbs - 30 g
- bacon bits - 30 g
- scallions - 30 g
- cheese, grated - 30 g
- salt - to taste
- black pepper - to taste
- butter - a spoonful
- cooking oil - a spoonful

### For garnish

- sour cream
- scallions
- bacon bits

## Method

### Step 1: Combine ingredients

Combine mashed potatoes, bacon bits, egg, scallions, cheese, salt, and pepper in a bowl and mix well.

Pour the breadcrumbs into a separate small bowl.

### Step 2: Shape & coat the potatoes

Shape the potato mixture into 3-inch patties and then dip them into the breadcrumbs until evenly coated.

### Step 3: Fry the pancakes & serve

Heat oil and butter in a nonstick skillet over medium heat and shallow fry the potato pancakes on both sides until they are heated through and browned, approximately 4 minutes on each side.

Serve with sour cream, scallions, and more bacon bits.
