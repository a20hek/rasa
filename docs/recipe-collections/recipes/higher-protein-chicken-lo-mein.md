## Ingredients

- Lo Mein Noodles, 150 g
- Neutral oil, as needed
- Chicken Breasts, 300 g
- Light soy sauce, a splash
- Chicken bouillon, a sprinkle
- Sesame Oil, a drizzle
- Shaoxing wine, a splash
- Light soy sauce, 25 g
- Oyster Sauce, 15 g
- Sugar, 10 g
- Chicken bouillon, 5 g
- Water, a splash
- Garlic, 2 cloves
- Fresh ginger, 1” knob
- Chinese broccoli, 1/2 bunch
- Yellow squash, 1/2
- Bell peppers, 1/2
- Red Onions, 1/2
- Bean sprouts, a handful

## Method

### Step 1: Boil the noodles & slice the chicken

Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook the noodles to the lower end of the package instructions.

You want them slightly underdone since they’ll finish cooking in the sauce.

Drain and set aside.

Meanwhile, slice the chicken breast into thin, bite-sized pieces.

### Step 2: Marinate the chicken

Add the chicken to a bowl with light soy sauce, chicken bouillon, sesame oil, and Shaoxing cooking wine.

Mix until evenly coated.

### Step 3: Mix the sauce

Combine light soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, chicken bouillon, and a splash of water and mix until everything is dissolved. Set aside.

### Step 4: Sear the chicken

Heat oil in a wok or pan over high heat.

Add the chicken and sear or stir-fry until lightly browned and just cooked through.

Remove and set aside.

### Step 5: Stir-fry the vegetables

Add a bit more oil if needed, then add garlic and ginger to bloom for a few seconds until fragrant.

Add Chinese broccoli, squash, bell pepper, and onion to stir-fry until lightly softened. If needed, do this in small batches to not overcrowd the pan.

### Step 6: Combine & toss the noodles

Add noodles and sauce. Toss until glossy and evenly coated.

Return the chicken and mix to combine.

Add bean sprouts at the end to keep them fresh and crisp.

Enjoy.
