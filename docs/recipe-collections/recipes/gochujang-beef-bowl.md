## Ingredients

- Ground beef, 2 parts
- Green Peas, 1 part
- Gochujang, 1/4 part
- Salt, a sprinkle
- Sesame Oil, a drizzle
- Water, as needed
- Rice
- Scallions, for garnish
- Pickled ginger, for garnish

## Method

### Step 1: Sear the beef

Add the ground beef to a pan over medium heat. Add a sprinkle of salt.

Let it render out some fat and sear. As it starts to brown, break it up with a wooden spoon or spatula.

### Step 2: Create the sauce

When the beef is almost cooked through, add the gochujang and peas (frozen peas work great here).

Mix until well combined, and add a splash of water if the mixture is too tight.

Stir & reduce until the sauce glazes the meat.

Turn off the heat and finish with a drizzle of sesame oil. Taste and adjust with more salt if needed.

### Step 3: Serve

Add a portion of the beef mixture over a scoop of cooked rice.

Optionally garnish with scallions & pickled ginger

Enjoy.
