## Ingredients

- Vite ramen, 1 pack
- Eggs, 1
- Cooking oil, a drizzle
- Gochujang, 10 g
- Kewpie mayo, 5 g
- Chinese Black Vinegar, 5 g
- Scallions
- Black sesame seeds

## Method

### Step 1: Boil the noodles

Boil the noodles according to package directions, and drain.

### Step 2: Mix the sauce & fry an egg

In a small bowl, mix together gochujang, mayo, and vinegar until smooth. Taste and adjust if needed.

Fry a sunny side up egg to your desired doneness and set aside.

### Step 3: Assemble & serve

In the noodle pot, mix together the drained noodles and the noodle sauce.

Add the noodles to a plate, and top it with a fried egg, sliced scallions, and black sesame seeds. Enjoy.
