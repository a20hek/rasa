# Egg Salad Framework

Source path: `/recipe/egg-salad-framework`

Easy & full of protein.

## Metadata

- Author: Keith McBrayer
- Yield: 1-2 servings
- Time: 10 mins
- Tags: Framework, Lazy, Quick
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/e70e94577b06f5851d5f3a07c1db90eb64c19f93-3543x3543.jpg`

## Ingredients

### 2 parts protein

- hard-boiled eggs - 4-5

### 1 part aromatics

- onions, diced - ~30 g
- celery, diced - ~30 g
- scallions - ~30 g

### 1/4 creamy

- mayonnaise - ~15 g
- olive oil - ~5 g

### 1/8 part acidic & flavor adjusters

- mustard - ~10 g
- lemon juice - to taste
- salt - to taste
- black pepper - to taste

### Serve with

- crackers
- sandwich bread

## Method

### Step 1: Prepare ingredients

Hard-boil the eggs if you don’t have any and prepare any crunchy aromatics.

Grab any flavor adjusters you want to use.

### Step 2: Toss & mash together

Add all components to a bowl and mash together until well combined and creamy to your desired texture.

Taste and adjust with more salt and acid (lemon juice or vinegar) as needed.

Serve on a sandwich, with crackers, or as a side dish.
