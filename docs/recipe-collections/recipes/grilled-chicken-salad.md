## Ingredients

- Chicken Thighs, 6
- Salt, 
- Kewpie mayo, 30 g
- Cumin, 6 g
- Paprika, 6 g
- Garlic Powder, 6g
- Turmeric powder, 6 g
- Sumac, 6 g
- Black Pepper, 6 g
- Ground cinnamon, a sprinkle
- Cayenne Pepper, a sprinkle
- Yogurt, 150 g
- Garlic, 2 cloves
- Black Pepper, a spoonful
- Lemon juice, 1/2 lemon
- Fresh dill, a spoonful
- Salt, to taste
- Romaine lettuce, 
- Cherry tomatoes, 
- Cucumbers, 
- Feta, 
- Pickled onions, 
- Tortilla strips, 

## Method

### Step 1: Marinate the chicken

Salt the chicken thighs with a even sprinkle of salt on all sides. In a small bowl, combine all of the spices with the mayo. Add the mayo marinade to the chicken and rub it all over.

### Step 2: Sear the chicken

To a ripping hot griddle or cast iron, add the chicken and sear until browned on both sides. Brown the chicken until it has reached around 170°F/ 76°C. Once the chicken is cooked, pull it off the heat and let it rest a bit before slicing.

### Step 3: Mix the dressing & prep the vegetables

Combine the yogurt, garlic, spices, and lemon juice. Mix it together and taste it. Adjust the salt and other seasonings to your preference.

### Step 4: Assemble the salad

Add the lettuce to a bowl and top with the tortilla strips or pita. Layer on the yogurt sauce, cucumber, tomato, feta, cheese, chicken, and pickled onion. Enjoy.
