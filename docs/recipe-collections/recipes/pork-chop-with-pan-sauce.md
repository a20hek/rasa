# Pork Chop with Pan Sauce

Source path: `/recipe/pork-chop-with-pan-sauce`

The perfect way to clean your pan.

## Ingredients

### The meat

- pork chops - 1-2 / 1 lb / 450 g
- cooking oil - a drizzle
- salt - to taste
- black pepper - to taste

### 1 parts deglazing liquid

- broth - ~200 g

### 1/4 part aromatics

- garlic, minced - ~15 g
- shallots, diced - ~15 g
- herbs, chopped - ~15 g

### 1/8 part thickener

- butter, cold - ~15 g
- mustard - ~10 g
- cornstarch slurry - a splash

## Method

### Step 1: Sear the pork chop

Season both sides of the pork chop with salt and pepper.

Set a pan over medium-high heat and add the oil. Sear the pork until you have color on both sides.

Remove the pan from the heat and let the pork chop rest. Allow it to finish cooking until it's about 10 degrees below your desired internal temperature.

- Optionally dry brine it in the refrigerator overnight.

### Step 2: Deglaze the pan

Add pan sauce aromatics to the remaining fat in the pan and sauté for a minute or so.

Add enough deglazing liquid of choice to cover the pan in a thin layer. Scrape any stuck fond off the bottom and bring to a simmer.

### Step 3: Thicken the sauce

Add your thickener of choice, and then gently simmer and reduce the sauce until it has arrived at your desired sauce consistency.

### Step 4: Serve & enjoy

The pork chop should be well-rested by now. Slice, and serve on a plate with a generous covering of warm pan sauce.
