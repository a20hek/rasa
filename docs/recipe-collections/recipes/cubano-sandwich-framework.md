# Cubano Sandwich Framework

Source path: `/recipe/cubano-sandwich-framework`

The building blocks of the iconic sandwich.

## Ingredients

### The bread

- cubano roll - 1
- butter - for toasting

### The cheese

- swiss - 2 slices

### The meats

- braised pork, shredded - 113 g / 1/4 lb
- ham, sliced - 2-4 slices

### The condiments

- pickles, sliced - 4 planks
- mustard - to taste

## Method

### Step 1: Gather & prep ingredients

Braise some pork if you don’t have any leftover. If you do have some leftover, go ahead and pull it out and slice it very thin or shred it.

Gather any remaining ingredients. Slice the Cubano bread.

- If you want more guidance, I make mojo braised pork here .

### Step 2: Prep & assemble the sandwich

Set a pan or griddle over medium heat.

Butter the outsides of the bread, add mustard to the insides, and then layer the remaining sandwich components and close.

- Be generous with the mustard and pickles. Their acidity balances the fatty pork and unctuous melty cheese.

### Step 3: Cook & press the sandwich

Cook and press the sandwich on the pan, flipping occasionally until both sides are golden brown, the inside ingredients are warmed through, and the cheese has fully melted.

Slice on a diagonal and enjoy warm.

- Alternatively, use a panini press if you have one.
