# 5 Minute Muhammara Dip

Source path: `/recipe/muhammara-dip`

Easy and filling Mediterranean red pepper dip.

## Ingredients

### Red pepper dip

- roasted red pepper condiment - 2 parts
- walnuts - 1/2 part
- breadcrumbs - 1/2 part
- olive oil - 1/2 part
- pomegranate molasses - 1/2 part
- salt - to taste

### For serving

- crackers
- pita bread
- raw vegetables

## Method

### Step 1: Blend, adjust, & serve

Blend all muhammara components until smooth. Adjust with more salt and flavor adjusters to preference. The texture should resemble hummus — thicken it up with more nuts/breadcrumbs, or thin it out with water.

Serve with crackers, pita, raw vegetables, or even on a sandwich as a condiment!
