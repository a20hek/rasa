# Chicken Salad Framework

Source path: `/recipe/chicken-salad-framework`

Great way to use up leftover chicken.

## Metadata

- Author: Keith McBrayer
- Time: 10 mins
- Tags: Framework, Feel Good, Quick
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/fc36b59152441babb6c3d4250b97cae9b22c2ffd-3543x3543.jpg`

## Ingredients

### 2 parts protein

- cooked chicken - ~226 g / 1/2 lb

### 1 part aromatics

- carrots - ~25 g
- celery - ~25 g
- onions - ~25 g
- scallions - ~25 g

### 1/4 creamy

- mayonnaise - ~25 g

### 1/8 part acidic & flavor adjusters

- mustard - ~12 g
- garlic powder - to taste
- paprika - to taste
- onion powder - to taste
- salt - to taste
- black pepper - to taste

## Method

### Step 1: Prepare the ingredients

Shred or dice the cooked chicken.

Prep any aromatics you want to throw in like carrots, celery, or onions.

### Step 2: Mix together & serve

Mix together all chicken salad components.

Adjust with more mayo if desired, more mustard for acidity, and salt + spices to taste. Serve with lettuce wraps, crackers, or bread.
