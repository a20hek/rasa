## Ingredients

- Cauliflower, 1 head
- Potatoes, 2-4
- Onions, 1/2
- Garlic, a spoonful
- Fresh ginger, a spoonful
- Green chilies, a spoonful
- Tomatoes, 1-2
- Cumin, a sprinkle
- Turmeric powder, a sprinkle
- Paprika, a sprinkle
- Garam Masala, a sprinkle
- Cooking oil, as needed
- Salt, as needed
- Water, as needed
- Cilantro, for garnish

## Method

### Step 1:  Toast the aromatics & spices

In a large pot, add some cooking oil over medium heat and saute all aromatics until softened and fragrant. Add in all ground spices and a pinch of salt, and toast for 1 minute longer.

### Step 2: Cook down the tomato

Add the tomato and cook until softened. If the mixture is dry and the spices or aromatics are at risk of burning, deglaze with a splash of water.

### Step 3: Coat the vegetables

Once the tomatoes have broken down, add in the cauliflower and potatoes. Add a generous sprinkle of salt to season. Toss until everything is well coated in the spice/sauce mixture.

Add in a heavy splash of water — just enough to create steam to cook the mixture.

### Step 4: Steam

Cover and let the vegetables steam until softened. This might take 10-20 minutes, but it depends on the batch size and pan setup.

Check and mix frequently - at least every 5 minutes.

Once they have reached your desired doneness — remove the cover and turn up the heat to evaporate any excess moisture.

### Step 5: Finish

Cut the heat and toss with chopped cilantro. Taste and adjust with more salt or spices, if needed.
