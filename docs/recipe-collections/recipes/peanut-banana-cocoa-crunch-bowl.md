# Peanut Banana Cocoa Crunch Bowl

Source path: `/recipe/peanut-banana-cocoa-crunch-bowl`

Lazy & healthy breakfast, snack, or dessert.

## Metadata

- Author: Ethan Chlebowski
- Time: 5 mins
- Tags: Quick, High Protein, Easy, Bad Day
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/b56df5ef8a46069fc96f640a13afa8dddd23cf2e-1500x1000.jpg`

## Ingredients

### Bowl components

- greek yogurt, plain, non-fat - 150 g / 2 parts
- bananas, frozen, chunks - 65 g / 1 part
- protein bars, crumbled - 1/2 / 1/2 part
- peanuts, salted - 14 g / a spoonful
- cocoa nibs - 6 g / a sprinkle
- honey - a drizzle

## Method

### Step 1: Layer ingredients & serve

Add yogurt to a bowl, followed by frozen banana chunks, protein bar cubes, peanuts, cocoa, and a drizzle of honey. Customize it how you like! Enjoy.
