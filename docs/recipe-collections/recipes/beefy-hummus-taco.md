# Beefy Hummus Taco

## Ingredients

- Ground beef, ~ 113g
- Beef bouillon paste, 2 g
- Water, 2 spoonfuls
- Salt, a sprinkle
- Za'atar, a sprinkle
- Cherry tomatoes, a handful
- Fresh dill, a sprinkle
- Sumac, a sprinkle
- Olive Oil, a drizzle
- Feta, to taste
- Whole wheat tortilla, 2
- Hummus, to taste

## Method

### Step 1: Brown the beef

Place a pan over medium high heat.

Add in the ground beef and smash it with your hand or a spatula into an even layer. Add a sprinkle of salt on top & let the beef brown for several minutes.

Once browned on the bottom, scrape it all up with a spatula and allow it to sear until cooked through. Add a sprinkle of za’atar to the beef.

### Step 2: Prep the tomato salad

While the beef is browning, add the halved tomatoes to a bowl and toss with fresh dill, sumac, feta cheese and a bit of olive oil. Stir and set aside.

### Step 3: Finish the beef

Add the bouillon and water to the beef mixture to deglaze. Stir until evenly coated, scraping up any browned bits on the bottom of the pan.

### Step 4: Assemble the taco

Add a drizzle of olive oil to the tortillas & toast on a hot pan or griddle.

Add a spoonful of hummus to the warmed tortillas. Next add a scoop of the beef followed by the tomato salad. Serve & devour!
