## Ingredients

- Shrimp, 227 g
- Mayonnaise, 2 spoonfuls
- Salt, a sprinkle
- Chipotles in Adobo, a spoonful
- Cumin, a sprinkle
- Corn tortillas, 4-6
- Low-fat sour cream, 
- Hot Sauce, 
- Salsa verde, 
- Pickled onions, 

## Method

### Step 1: Marinate the shrimp

Add the peeled shrimp to a bowl. Mix with the salt, mayo, ground cumin, and blended chipotles in adobo. Set aside for a few minutes to marinate.

So long as the shrimp are peeled, they can be cooked straight from frozen!

Make sure to prepare/gather all your other taco components before you cook the shrimp because they will cook fast.

### Step 2: Sear the shrimp

Set a large skillet or griddle over high heat.

Once the griddle is hot, toss on the shrimp. Sear them until browned and cooked through about 6-8 minutes.

### Step 3: Assemble & serve

Remove the shrimp and toast the corn tortillas on the warm pan.

Slather the tortillas with sour cream & hot sauce, the cooked shrimp, salsa verde, and pickled onions. Enjoy.
