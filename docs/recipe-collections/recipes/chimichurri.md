# Chimichurri

Source path: `/recipe/chimichurri`

The condiment of the summer. 

## Metadata

- Author: Ethan Chlebowski
- Time: 15 mins
- Tags: Framework, Easy, Quick
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/86551051d0eb673631290f6b9540a657388bdb33-1312x1108.png`

## Ingredients

### Herbs of choice

- parsley, minced - 1 bunch

### Fat source

- extra virgin olive oil - 3 parts / 75 g

### Acid source

- red wine vinegar - 1 part / 25 g

### Aromatics & spices

- garlic, minced - 2 cloves
- dried oregano - a sprinkle
- red pepper flakes - a sprinkle
- black pepper - a few cranks
- salt - to taste

## Method

### Step 1: Combine ingredients & adjust

Finely chop the parsley and mince the garlic. Gather all remaining ingredients.

Add parsley and garlic to a bowl, then add the olive oil and red wine vinegar, which should barely submerge the mixture. If you want a looser consistency, add a bit more oil and vinegar. Sprinkle in the dried oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper.

Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed. Enjoy over everything.
