# Menemen

Source path: `/recipe/menemen`

An easy weeknight meal and a great way to use up eggs.

## Metadata

- Author: Keith McBrayer
- Yield: 2-4 servings
- Time: 20 mins
- Tags: Easy, Quick, Eggs
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/d773ec887b1987b4d5c7e07268cf92dc27e158d7-1292x1292.jpg`

## Ingredients

### Menemen Base

- eggs - 3-5
- tomatoes, diced - 2-3
- green bell peppers, diced - 1
- salt - to taste
- extra virgin olive oil - a drizzle

### Aromatics & spices

- onions, diced, optional - 1/2
- paprika - a sprinkle
- dried oregano - a sprinkle

### Additional toppings

- extra virgin olive oil
- parsley
- dill

## Method

### Step 1: Sauté the vegetables & spices

In a wide pan, sauté the peppers, onions, tomatoes, and spices with oil and a pinch of salt until aromatic and softened.

### Step 2: Add the eggs

Lightly beat the eggs together. Add the egg mixture to the aromatic base. Gently fold the mixture together as it sets so the vegetables don’t become too homogenous with the eggs.

### Step 3: Finish & serve

Once the eggs are mostly cooked through, remove from the heat. Taste and adjust with salt. Garnish with herbs, if you are using them, and a healthy drizzle of olive oil.
