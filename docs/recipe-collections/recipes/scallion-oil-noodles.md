# Scallion Oil Noodles

Source path: `/recipe/scallion-oil-noodles`

A quick lunch to have on hand.

## Metadata

- Author: Keith McBrayer
- Yield: 2 servings
- Time: 10 mins
- Tags: Easy, Lazy, Quick
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/245e7000ee36cf1d0dbbf7a605c73f02dcf7940a-3543x3543.jpg`

## Ingredients

### Noodle components

- chinese wheat noodles - 200 g / 2 parts
- neutral oil - 50 g / 1/2 part
- scallions - 50 g / 1/2 part

### Sauce components

- light soy sauce - 15 g / a spoonful
- dark soy sauce - 15 g / a spoonful
- sugar - 10 g

## Method

### Step 1: Cook the noodles

Cook the noodles and drain them. Have all your ingredients ready to go.

Heat a wok or large pan over high heat.

### Step 2: Fry the scallions

Set a pan or wok over medium heat.

Add the oil and scallions. Fry the scallions until they are beginning to turn brown, stirring frequently.

### Step 3: Add the noodles

Add the drained noodles and toss to combine.

Add both soy sauces around the edges of the wok so that they sizzle and caramelize some, then add in sugar. Toss until the noodles are evenly coated.

### Step 4: Taste & enjoy

Taste for seasoning and add more light soy sauce or sugar, to taste. Serve immediately.
