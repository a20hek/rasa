## Ingredients

- Chicken Breasts, ~2
- Spice rub of choice, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- Cooking oil, for searing
- Jasmine Rice, 1 part
- Water, 1.25 parts
- Chicken bouillon, a spoonful
- Turmeric powder, a sprinkle

- Aleppo pepper, a sprinkle
- Cooking oil, a drizzle
- Low-fat sour cream, 3 parts
- Mayonnaise, 1 part
- Dried dill, a sprinkle
- Lemon juice, a squeeze
- Lemon zest, a pinch
- Black Pepper, to taste

- Iceberg lettuce, 
- Cucumbers, 
- Pickled onions,

## Method

### Step 1: Start the rice

Add the rice, water, chicken bouillon, turmeric, cloves, and aleppo pepper to a rice cooker.

Mix everything together so the spices are evenly dispersed, then start cooking.

If you don't have a rice cooker, you can steam the rice on the stove or use your favorite rice cooking method.

### Step 2: Prep vegetables and sauce

Thinly slice the iceberg lettuce for a light, crunchy base. Halve the cherry tomatoes, chop your cucumbers, and gather pickled onions and hot sauce, if using.

In a bowl, combine sour cream, mayo, dried dill, lemon zest, lemon juice, and black pepper. Mix until smooth, taste, and adjust if needed.

### Step 3: Cube & sear the chicken

Cut the chicken breast into small, even cubes.

Smaller pieces = faster cooking and more surface area for seasoning.

Heat a pan over high heat, then add a small drizzle of oil. Add the chicken and let it sear undisturbed to develop browning.

Once one side is browned, add your spice rub (+ salt, if your spice mix isn’t salty) and toss to coat.

Continue searing until fully cooked through, about 4–5 minutes total. Reduce heat or add a splash of water if anything threatens to burn.

Taste and adjust final seasoning with salt, if needed.

### Step 4: Fluff & finish the rice with oil

Optional: Once the rice is done, drizzle in about 10 g of oil before fluffing.

Mix the cooked rice thoroughly to coat the grains and distribute the oil.

The fat enhances texture and helps carry the spice aromas.

### Step 5: Assemble and serve

Start with a base of yellow rice. Add the chicken to one side, then layer on lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, and pickled onions to taste.

Drizzle the dill sauce over the chicken and finish with hot sauce if desired. Enjoy.
