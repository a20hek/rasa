# Aglio e Olio

Source path: `/recipe/aglio-e-olio`

Simple pantry pasta.

## Metadata

- Author: Keith McBrayer
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 30 mins
- Tags: Easy, Bad Day, Quick
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/177a7ea510896ec07e02a6023978323ee016ebf5-3543x3543.jpg`

## Ingredients

### Pasta components

- spaghetti - 225 g / 1/2 lb
- extra virgin olive oil - 60 g
- garlic, thinly sliced - 3-6 cloves
- red pepper flakes - to taste
- parsley, finely chopped - small handful

## Method

### Step 1: Boil the spaghetti

Bring a pot of water to boil. Add a large sprinkle of salt.

Boil the spaghetti until al dente.

- Reserve a bit of pasta water before draining.

### Step 2: Sauté the aromatics

Add the oil to the skillet.

Sauté the sliced garlic until golden brown, then add the red pepper flakes & bloom them for a few seconds.

### Step 3: Emulsify & serve

Add the cooked pasted to the pan, and toss with a few drizzles of pasta water until the oil forms a light emulsion and clings to the noodles.

Sprinkle with chopped parsley and serve warm. Enjoy!
