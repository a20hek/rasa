## Ingredients

- 93/7 ground beef, 450 g
- Tomato Paste, 7 g
- Salt, 7 g
- Cumin, 3 g
- Smoked Paprika, 3 g
- Cayenne Pepper, 2 g
- Yogurt, 50 g
- Green chile sauce, 15 g
- Lime juice, a drizzle
- Tortilla Chips, ~10
- Monterrey jack cheese, 30 g
- Red Onions, 
- Pickled jalapeños, 
- Romaine lettuce, 

## Method

### Step 1: Brown the beef

In a pan, add the ground beef, salt, and spices. Use a wooden spoon or potato masher to break up the beef as it cooks over medium-high heat. Once the beef is well browned, add in the tomato paste along with a splash of water and mix until the beef is glazed.

### Step 2: Make the sauce

In a small bow, mix together yogurt, green chile sauce, and lime juice until homogenous and desired spice/acidity is reached.

### Step 3: Assemble the bowl

Chop the veggies to your desired size, and grate the cheese. To assemble, add the lettuce as a base, then follow with 1/3 of the beef, grated cheese, pickled jalapeños, red onion, and the green chile yogurt sauce. Mix it all up and then eat it with a serving tortilla chips.
