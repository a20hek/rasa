## Ingredients

- White Cheddar Powder, 30 g
- Skim milk, 30 g
- Low-fat sour cream, 30 g
- Dried pasta, 170 g
- Salt, to taste
- chicken, 150 g
- Frozen peas, 100 g

## Method

### Step 1: Boil the pasta

In a saucepot or wide pan, barely submerge the dried pasta with water and add a sprinkle of salt. Boil until most of the water has been absorbed by the pasta, and the pasta is al dente, about 12-13 minutes.

This will concentrate the starch from the paste and help create a sauce without needing extra butter and cheese.

### Step 2: Make the sauce

Turn off the heat. To create the sauce, add the cheddar powder, milk, and sour cream and stir until creamy.

### Step 3: Add ingredients & serve

Eat the mac as is, or add peas & chicken until warmed through. Enjoy.
