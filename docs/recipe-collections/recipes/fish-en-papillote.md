# Fish en Papillote

Source path: `/recipe/fish-en-papillote`

Poached fish in parchment paper.

## Metadata

- Author: Keith McBrayer
- Yield: 2 servings
- Time: 25 mins
- Tags: Easy, Feel Good, Quick
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/4f3ab63f578265368e693f56cd859e1ceded6329-3543x3543.jpg`

## Ingredients

### 2 parts fish

- cod - ~ 170 g / 1 fillet

### 1 part vegetables

- green beans, thinly sliced - ~ 85 g

### Aromatics

- citrus slices - to taste
- herbs - to taste
- garlic - to taste
- scallions - to taste

### Poaching components

- broth, or white wine - a splash
- salt - a sprinkle

## Method

### Step 1: Prepare the parchment paper

Cut parchment paper into a large circle.

Place vegetables, aromatics, and a filet on one half of the parchment. Sprinkle with salt.

Fold the parchment over and seal the edges to create a package. Add cooking liquid before sealing completely.

Repeat for remaining servings.

### Step 2: Bake the fish

Transfer parchment boats to a cooking sheet, then bake at 190°C for 10-15 minutes or until the vegetables are tender and the fish is cooked through.

### Step 3: Serve

Taste & adjust with an extra sprinkle of salt, if needed.

Transfer packages to plates. You can serve the meal right in its parchment package. Enjoy.
