## Ingredients

- Beef chuck, ~ 3 lb
- Salt, as needed
- Neutral oil, a spoonful
- Garlic, 5 cloves
- Shallots, 2-3
- Fresh ginger, 2 spoonfuls
- Turmeric powder, 2 spoonfuls
- Galangal Root, 2 spoonfuls
- Cumin, a spoonful
- Ground coriander, a spoonful
- Coconut Flakes, a spoonful
- Coconut Milk, ~ 700 g
- Lemongrass, 2-3 stalks
- Makrut lime leaves, 2-3

## Method

### Step 1: Salt & sear the beef

Generously season the meat chunks with salt on each side.

Heat a Dutch oven or pot over medium-high heat and add the oil. Sear the beef in batches until well browned.

### Step 2: Make the aromatic paste

In a mortar & pestle or food processor, puree aromatics into a rough paste.

Add to the braising pot with the meat, making sure it’s enough to coat each piece after mixing. Stir and saute the mixture until the paste starts to caramelize and cling to each piece.

Sprinkle the ground spices over top.

### Step 3: Add the remaining ingredients & braise

Add the whole lemongrass, lime leaves, and coconut milk - enough to just cover the meat.

Cover the Dutch oven and pop into a 300°F/148°C oven to braise for 3-5 hours or until the meat is completely fork tender.

Let it rest and cool out of the oven for at least an hour.

### Step 4: Shred & serve

Shred the meat to your desired consistency.

Taste and adjust with more salt, if needed. Serve over rice!
