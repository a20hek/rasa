# Whipped Feta Dip

Source path: `/recipe/whipped-feta`

A light summer snack or lunch.

## Metadata

- Author: Keith McBrayer
- Yield: 2-4 servings
- Time: 5 mins
- Tags: Easy, Quick, Feel Good
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/11c8b66f95872aefe684b34dee1ba22a210f48e1-3543x3543.jpg`

## Ingredients

### Whipped feta

- feta - 2 parts / 200 g
- greek yogurt - 1/4 part / 25 g

### Garnish

- olive oil
- garlic, roasted
- nuts, toasted
- olives
- lemon zest

### Dipping options

- pita bread
- crackers
- raw vegetables

## Method

### Step 1: Assemble & serve

In a blender, add feta chunks and a small amount of a greek yogurt to adjust the texture. Blend until smooth and aerated, adding more yogurt or a splash of water (if needed) to loosen it up. Taste and adjust with salt or lemon juice for extra acidity.

Serve with garnishes and dipping items of choice.
