# Cheesy & Garlicky Roasted Broccoli

Source path: `/recipe/cheesy-and-garlicky-roasted-broccoli`

Simple freezer broccoli side.

## Metadata

- Author: Ethan Chlebowski
- Yield: 4 servings
- Time: 45 mins
- Tags: Quick, Easy, Lazy
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/4a1c3773443257a3d4f2c32d543358bb9deefaae-1500x1500.jpg`

## Ingredients

### Broccoli

- broccoli, frozen - 1 bag
- extra virgin olive oil - as needed

### Aromatics & spices

- garlic - 5 cloves
- salt - to taste
- black pepper - to taste
- parmesan, grated - to taste

## Method

### Step 1: Prep the pan & broccoli

Preheat the oven to 246°C. Add a drizzle of oil to coat a sheet pan and place in the oven to preheat.

In a bowl, toss together the frozen broccoli, salt, garlic cloves, black pepper, and enough olive oil to coat.

### Step 2: Roast

Once the oven is hot, pull the pan out, add the tossed vegetables, and evenly space them out across the hot pan. Roast for 30 to 40 minutes until deeply browned.

Taste and adjust seasoning as needed. Optionally serve with grated cheese over the top. Enjoy.
