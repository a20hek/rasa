# Low Calorie Weeknight Burger

Source path: `/recipe/low-calorie-smash-burger`

A quick and easy lower calorie burger. 

## Metadata

- Author: Ethan Chlebowski
- Yield: 1 serving
- Time: 10 mins
- Tags: Easy, Quick, High Protein
- Image: `https://cdn.sanity.io/images/g1s4qnmz/production/a7949b32a673769dbf59a4e50504958219b7f249-3499x3499.jpg`

## Ingredients

### Beef patty

- ground beef, 90/10 - 125 g
- american cheese - 1 slice
- salt

### Bun & toppings

- hamburger buns - 1
- ketchup
- dijon mustard
- red onions, diced
- pickles, sliced
- lettuce, shredded

## Method

### Step 1: Sear & smash the patty

Heat a griddle or a pan to high heat.

Shape the ground beef into a ball and place on the griddle. Sprinkle salt on top of the patty and smash it into the griddle, making it flat.

Sear on one side until browned and crispy. Flip over the patty and place the slice of cheese on top. Cook the patty until browned on the other side, and the cheese has begun to melt.

### Step 2: Assemble the burger

Add a squirt of ketchup and dijon to the bun and spread evenly. Add the diced onions, pickles, lettuce, and the cooked patty. Close the burger and enjoy.
