# Spicy Thai Meatballs

Source path: `/recipe/spicy-thai-meatballs`

Thai inspired spicy meatballs.

- Author: Ethan Chlebowski

## Ingredients

### Aromatics

- birds eye green chilies - 3-5
- thai basil - 1 bunch
- shallots - 1

### Meat & seasoning

- ground pork - 450 g / 1 lb
- hoisin - 25 g
- fish sauce - 10 g
- dark soy sauce - 5 g
- salt - 2 g
- white pepper - 2 g
- msg - 1 g
- water - a splash

## Method

### Step 1: Prep the aromatics

Prep the aromatics.

Chop the basil, dice the shallot and bird’s eye chilis.

### Step 2: Mix the meatballs

Preheat the oven to 450°F/232°C.

In a large bowl, mix together the meatball components along with the aromatics. Mix until everything is evenly distributed.

### Step 3: Form the meatballs & bake

Put a little water on your hands to prevent sticking. Form the meatballs into golf ball sized balls and place on a sheet pan.

Roast in the oven for 15-20 minutes, or until browned and cooked through.

Enjoy right away or store them in the fridge for later use.
