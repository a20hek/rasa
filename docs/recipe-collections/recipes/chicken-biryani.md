## Method

### Step 1: Fry the aromatics & marinate the chicken

Set a cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Add the vegetable oil.

While heating, finely slice half an onion into rings. Add the bay leaves and fry for 15 seconds. Toss in the sliced onions and fry for 8-10 minutes until lightly browned. Be careful not to burn them!

Meanwhile, add the marinade ingredients (except the yogurt and chicken) to a food processor and pulse until smooth. Place the marinade paste, yogurt, and chicken in a bowl and mix to combine.

### Step 2: Simmer the chicken

Remove half of the fried onions with a slotted spoon and set aside. Reserve some of the oil and bay leaves for garnish later on.

Add the chicken and marinade to the cast iron and simmer for 8 minutes until the marinade has reduced by half.

Meanwhile, set a large pot of water on the stove and bring to a boil.

### Step 3: Par-boil the rice & soak the turmeric

Preheat the oven to 300°F/148°C.

Add the rice to the boiling water and parboil the rice for 8-10 minutes, then drain in a colander.

Soak the turmeric in hot but not boiling milk (warm it up in the microwave or in a saucepot).

### Step 4: Bake the biryani

Remove the chicken from the stove and layer the rice over top. Pour the turmeric milk over the rice in orange lines. Spoon the onion-flavored oil from the pan along with the bay leaves.

Cover the dish with aluminum foil and place the lid over top to seal the dish.

Bake for 10-15 minutes or until the rice has absorbed any extra liquid and is cooked through and fluffy.

Spoon the biryani onto a plate and sprinkle with fried onions and other garnishes of your choice. Enjoy.
